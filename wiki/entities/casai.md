# Casai

A proptech startup (short/mid-term rental management, per context) that,
per [[creando-unicornios]], **no longer exists** — cited in the book as a
cautionary counter-example inside the chapter on the broader startup
ecosystem, not one of the book's main unicorn-profile chapters.

**Confidence note**: this is a single secondhand account (a Kindle
highlight of the book's retelling, itself quoting or paraphrasing an
unnamed founder's reflections). Treat as one account of what happened, not
a verified post-mortem — no independent source is cross-checked here.

## What the source says went wrong (founder's own retrospective, as quoted)

- Underinvestment in financial infrastructure/tooling early on.
- Underinvestment in the people/HR side — HR was reportedly viewed as
  unnecessary at the time; "improvising" is called out as unproductive
  leadership.
- Spent/committed capital as if more funding would always be available,
  rather than operating as if capital were scarce.
- The founder names a specific failure mode: **"la falacia del CEO"** —
  the belief that "nadie más puede entender o empatizar" (nobody else can
  understand or empathize with the specific pressures), which stopped them
  from asking for help with both business problems and their own mental
  health.

## Relevance to this wiki

Cited in [[creando-unicornios]] alongside

behind that theme. No connection to [[athletix-ai]] beyond being a
