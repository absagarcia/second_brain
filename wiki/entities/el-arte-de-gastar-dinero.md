# El arte de gastar dinero (The Art of Spending Money)

Morgan Housel (recalled attribution), ~2025. Not about how to *make* money but
how to *spend* it well — psychology over spreadsheets. **Long-lived**
(principles; the specific price anecdotes are illustrative).

## Core ideas

- **Personal finance is more personal than finance.** "Todo comportamiento tiene
  sentido cuando dispones de suficiente información" — people spend differently
  because they've lived differently; don't judge how others spend. Spending or
  saving against your own personality is "como vivir la vida forzándote a ser
  alguien que no eres."
- **Wealth = what you have minus what you want.** "La mejor forma de calcular la
  riqueza es sumar lo que tienes y restarle lo que quieres." Happiness is
  satisfaction; **aim for durable satisfaction over fleeting happiness spikes.**
  The insatiability trap: "es improbable que tengas una vida buena... a menos que
  venzas tu insaciabilidad." Directly extends [[sapiens]]'s happiness-ceiling /
  luxuries-become-necessities observation and [[esencialismo]]'s "enough."
- **Spending as attention-seeking.** Much luxury spending buys *others'*
  attention — fast, visible, but shallow and short-lived. Respect earned by *who
  you are* rather than *what you own* collapses the urge to spend on status.
  Buffett: measure success by "cuántas de las personas que quieres que te quieran
  te quieren de verdad." Same nerve as [[atrevete-a-no-gustar]] (deny the desire
  for recognition) and [[minimalismo-digital]] (FOMO, likes).
- **Independence is wealth.** "La riqueza sin independencia es una forma peculiar
  de pobreza"; a ladder from total dependence up to passive income covering basic
  costs. Saving buys **independence today**, not just a future. Converges with
  [[investment-bucket-strategy]] and [[principios-nuevo-orden-mundial]] (savings/
  low-debt resilience).
- **Discreet accumulation**: internal reference points over external ones; "la
  riqueza rápida es riqueza frágil"; rich-and-anonymous (Naval) over rich-and-seen.
- **Minimize future regret.** "El único buen consejo es 'minimiza los
  remordimientos futuros'." Risk = regret, not volatility. Good memories are the
  closest thing to living in the present while saving for the future.
- **Utility vs. status**, and **$3 vs. $30k questions**: buying for utility
  expresses your identity, chasing status conforms you to others'; watch the big
  five budget lines (college, housing, car, health insurance, childcare) — "lo
  demás es un error de redondeo" — yet small leaks still matter. "Ahorra como un
  pesimista e invierte como un optimista."
