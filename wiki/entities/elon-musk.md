# Elon Musk (biografía de Isaacson)

Walter Isaacson's biography (recalled attribution), 2023. The highlights skew
heavily toward Musk's **operating method** rather than the life narrative —
which is the useful part for this wiki. **Long-lived** (method/principles); the
company-stage details are historical context.

## The Algorithm (Musk's 5-step method)

The most transferable artifact in the book — Musk's fixed order of operations,
which he had teams recite:

1. **Question every requirement.** Each must carry the *name* of a real person,
   never a department. "Las especificaciones indicadas por las personas
   inteligentes son las más peligrosas, porque... es menos probable que alguien
   las cuestione." Then make them less dumb.
2. **Delete parts / process steps.** "Si no acabas viéndote obligado a incluir de
   nuevo al menos un 10% de ellas, es que no habías eliminado las suficientes."
3. **Simplify and optimize** — but only *after* deleting, or you'll polish a step
   that shouldn't exist.
4. **Accelerate cycle time** — only after the first three.
5. **Automate — last.** The Nevada/Fremont mistake was automating first.

This delete-before-you-optimize ordering is the engineering twin of
[[esencialismo]] ("less but better") and [[effortless-action-principles]], and
the reason it earns its own concept page: [[first-principles-and-the-algorithm]].

## First-principles thinking & related operating beliefs

- **First principles.** "Si el pensamiento convencional hace que tu misión sea
  imposible... piensa más allá de lo convencional." Reason from physics, not
  analogy — the only rules are the laws of physics; "todo lo demás son
  recomendaciones."
- **Mission first, then make the finances work.** "Elon parte de una misión y más
  tarde encuentra una forma de rellenarla para que funcione en términos
  financieros." Contrast with [[monopoly-vs-competition-zero-to-one]] (secret/
  monopoly first) and [[creando-unicornios]] ("gana el que ejecuta") — three
  different starting points, recorded as differences in emphasis.
- **Build the machine that builds the machine.** "Lo que conduce al éxito no es el
  producto. Es la capacidad de fabricar ese producto de forma eficaz."
- **Obsessive sense of urgency** as an end in itself; "probar ideas novedosas y
  estar dispuestos a volar las cosas por los aires"; the **"índice del idiota"**
  (component cost ÷ raw-material cost) to find waste.
- **Hire for attitude; skills can be learned** ("los cambios de actitud exigen un
  trasplante de cerebro") — the inverse emphasis to career-capital-first
  [[hazlo-tan-bien-que-no-puedan-ignorarte]], noted as a tension.
- **Feedback on the action, not the person** ("hardcore y en su mayoría preciso...
  criticar la acción, no a la persona") — resonates with [[atrevete-a-no-gustar]]'s
  "ni elogios ni reprimendas" horizontal-relationship stance, from a very
  different temperament.
- Design connects form to function (the Jobs/Ive principle); possessions as "un
  peso... y un vector para ser atacado" (echoes [[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]]).
