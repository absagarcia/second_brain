# Atrévete a no gustar (The Courage to Be Disliked)

Ichiro Kishimi & Fumitake Koga (recalled attribution) — a Socratic dialogue
popularizing **Adlerian psychology**. **Long-lived** (a philosophy of living).

## Core Adlerian moves

- **Teleology over etiology ("el trauma no existe").** We are pulled by
  self-set *purposes*, not pushed by past causes. "Las personas no están
  controladas por causas pasadas, sino que avanzan hacia propósitos que se han
  fijado ellas mismas." Not "what happened?" but "how did you resolve it?" This
  is the deepest tension in the wiki's psychology cluster: it pulls *against*
  [[generacion-dopamina]]'s neurobiological, cause-and-mechanism account of
  behavior. Recorded as a genuine clash of frames — one says behavior is chosen
  toward a goal, the other says it's driven by a dopamine set-point.
- **"No es que te falte capacidad, sino que te falta valor"** — unhappiness is
  framed as a lack of *courage*, not ability. Compare [[la-guerra-del-arte]]
  (Resistance) and [[mindset-la-actitud-del-exito]].
- **All problems are interpersonal.** Inferiority is a *subjective* comparison;
  the healthy version compares to your **ideal self, not to others** ("la vida no
  es una competición") — the same internal-standard idea as
  [[de-que-hablo-cuando-hablo-de-correr]] and [[haz-cosas-dificiles]].
- **Separation of tasks.** Always ask "¿de quién es esta tarea?" and stop
  carrying other people's. Others' approval or disappointment is *their* task,
  not yours — "lo único que puede hacer es vivir solo en el universo... pero eso
  es imposible," so the work is boundaries, not isolation.
- **Deny the desire for recognition.** Chasing praise hands your self-worth to
  others; the **courage to be disliked** is the price of freedom. Directly echoed
  by [[haz-cosas-dificiles]]'s "silent ego" and by [[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]]'s
  warning about buying status for others' attention.
- **Neither praise nor scold; encourage.** Praise is "el juicio que una persona
  capaz hace acerca de una persona incapaz" — a vertical relationship. Build
  **horizontal** ones, where gratitude and respect arise directly.
- **Work as contribution, not money.** Its essence is feeling "le soy útil a
  alguien." **Work-addiction is a "life-lie"** — using work to dodge the rest of
  life. A psychological complement to the [[founder-mental-health-startup-risk]]
  thread and the anti-hustle line in [[sin-esfuerzo]] / [[esencialismo]].
- **Live intensely in the here and now** ("baila... los momentos del aquí y
  ahora"); "alguien tiene que empezar," regardless of whether others cooperate.
