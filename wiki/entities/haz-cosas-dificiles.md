# Haz cosas difíciles (Do Hard Things)

Steve Magness (recalled attribution), ~2022 — a running coach / sport scientist
redefining "toughness" against the macho model. Given Magness's background and
the exercise-based practices, this is the most **sports-performance**-adjacent
book in the wiki after [[system-design-interview]]'s infra content — relevant to
[[athletix-ai]]'s domain. **Long-lived** (principles + drills).

## Real toughness ≠ false bravado

> "La verdadera fortaleza no consiste en aprender a soportar el dolor... sino en
> convertirse en un ser humano más sano y feliz."

Toughness is creating **space between stimulus and response** and steering toward
the right choice, not the fastest one: "Sensación → Debate interno → Impulso →
Decisión." Four pillars:

1. **Ditch the façade, embrace reality.** Toughness = accurate read of the demands
   **+** accurate read of your abilities. Set **authentic goals just beyond current
   capacity** (the flow / growth-mindset zone). Stress read as *challenge, not
   threat* — the same reframe as [[mindset-la-actitud-del-exito]] and
   [[la-guerra-del-arte]]'s "fear points toward the work."
2. **Quiet confidence; insecurity is loud.** Real confidence is a **silent ego**;
   chasing self-esteem from others' approval guarantees low self-esteem — the same
   nerve as [[atrevete-a-no-gustar]] (deny recognition) and
   [[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]] (status). Improve your *best average*, not your
   unrepeatable peak — an internal standard, cf. [[de-que-hablo-cuando-hablo-de-correr]].
3. **Emotions are messengers, not dictators.** Feel them, interpret, contextualize;
   respond, don't react.
4. **Transcend discomfort via meaning.** "El sentido es el pegamento que mantiene
   unida nuestra mente" (Frankl-adjacent); toughness rests on met basic needs
   (voice, agency, progress, belonging), and Rendimiento = Esfuerzo real /
   Esfuerzo esperado × Motivación.

## Practical toolkit

- **Create space / sit with boredom.** Deliberately practice being alone with your
  thoughts (build up to 15–20 min), noticing the urge to grab the phone without
  obeying it — the same solitude prescription as [[minimalismo-digital]] and the
  discomfort-tolerance of [[generacion-dopamina]].
- **Widen vs. narrow focus** on demand (visual portrait/landscape, "raro vs.
  normal" thinking, posture, future-self time horizon, 2nd/3rd-person self-talk,
  environment) to escape negative spirals.
- **"El estado de ánimo sigue a la acción."** Behavior shifts mood, thought, and
  perception — the action-first mechanism underlying [[four-laws-of-behavior-change]].
- Names **burnout** as a Western epidemic (~76% of US workers cited) — an
  independent reinforcement of [[founder-mental-health-startup-risk]].
