# Minimalismo digital (Digital Minimalism)

Cal Newport (recalled attribution), ~2019 — the fourth Newport book in the wiki
([[deep-work]], [[hazlo-tan-bien-que-no-puedan-ignorarte]], and this).
**Long-lived** (philosophy + practices).

## Thesis

> "Una filosofía del uso de la tecnología por la que concentras el tiempo en
> línea en una pequeña cantidad de actividades cuidadosamente elegidas y
> optimizadas que apoyan sólidamente las cosas que más valoras."

Less-but-better applied to technology — the same discipline as [[esencialismo]],
pointed at tools. Two principles: **acumular sale caro** (many apps net-negative
even if each has small benefits) and **optimizar importa** (deciding a tool is
worthwhile is only step one; you must engineer *how* you use it). Thoreau's cost
accounting: "el coste de algo es la cantidad de vida que debo entregar a cambio."

## Key ideas & practices

- **The attention economy is an unbalanced arms race**: tech firms engineer
  behavioral addiction via **intermittent positive reinforcement + the desire for
  social approval** — the mechanism [[generacion-dopamina]] describes chemically.
- **The digital declutter**: 30-day step-back, then reintroduce only tools that
  clearly support something you value.
- **Solitude deprivation**: a state of never being alone with your own thoughts.
  Solitude is subjective (mind free of other minds' input), and its loss is
  corrosive — the same prescription as [[haz-cosas-dificiles]] ("crea espacio")
  and [[de-que-hablo-cuando-hablo-de-correr]] ("un tiempo de silencio sólo para
  mí"; "correr es más barato que ir al psicólogo" appears in *both* books).
- **Don't click "like"** — low-bandwidth approval substituting for real
  connection.
- **High-quality leisure** (three lessons): prioritize *demanding* activity over
  passive consumption; use skills to **produce things in the physical world**;
  seek activities requiring **structured real-world social interaction**. Plus
  the striking observation: "es imposible construir un imperio como Facebook si
  cada día pierdes horas usando Facebook."
- **Plan your leisure** with a *seasonal* and a *weekly* plan, scheduling even
  low-quality leisure in advance — a concrete **[[systems-over-willpower]]**
  practice extended into free time (this book is now a 7th independent source in
  that cluster on system/ritual design beating in-the-moment willpower).
