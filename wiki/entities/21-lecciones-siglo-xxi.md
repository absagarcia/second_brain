# 21 Lecciones para el Siglo XXI (21 Lessons for the 21st Century)

Widely known to be by Yuval Noah Harari (2018) — same caveat as the other
book ingests: not stated in the raw file itself (a Kindle highlights
export, no metadata page), so this attribution is recalled general
knowledge, not source-traceable.

**Nature of this source**: personal Kindle highlights export, very sparse
(5 highlights spanning Parts I, IV, and V of the book) — similar in
sparseness to [[vida-3-0]]. Treat this page as scattered pointers, not a
summary of the book's actual argument.

## What the highlighted passages cover

- **Intelligence vs. consciousness (Ch. 3, "Libertad")**: "La
  inteligencia es la capacidad de resolver problemas. La conciencia es la
  capacidad de sentir dolor, alegría, amor e ira." (Intelligence is the
  capacity to solve problems; consciousness is the capacity to feel pain,
  joy, love, and anger.) Extracted as part of a comparison, see
  [[intelligence-vs-consciousness-tegmark-harari]], since this wiki
  already has [[vida-3-0]]'s (Tegmark) intelligence definition.
- **Post-truth (Ch. 17)**: "Las naciones y las religiones son clubes de
  fútbol que han tomado esteroides" — used to argue that large-scale
  human cooperation runs on shared fictions, not necessarily literal
  truth, in the same way sports-club loyalty does. The same author makes
  a more developed version of this argument in [[sapiens]] — see
  [[imagined-orders-shared-fictions]].
- **Meditation (Ch. 21)**: defined broadly as "any method of direct
  observation of our own mind" — framed in the context of the book's
  "resiliencia" section.
- **Algorithmic identity (Ch. 21, same section)**: "serán los algoritmos
  los que decidan por nosotros quiénes somos y lo que deberíamos saber
  sobre nosotros" (algorithms will decide for us who we are and what we
  should know about ourselves) — stated as a concern, not elaborated
  further in what's highlighted.
- Chapter 20 ("Significado. La vida no es un relato") appears only as a
  bookmark with no highlighted text.

## Relevance to this wiki

Tagged `masters` alongside [[vida-3-0]] as general AI/tech-philosophy
reading. The intelligence/consciousness distinction is the most
reusable, comparable piece — see the comparison page for how it differs
from Tegmark's framing. See also [[sapiens]], the same author's earlier
book, ingested independently in this wiki.
