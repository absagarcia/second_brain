# Essentialism ("Less but Better") as a Prioritization Discipline

**Long-lived framework**: a prioritization philosophy from
[[esencialismo]] (Greg McKeown), not tied to any specific market/data
point, so it doesn't decay the way a stat or valuation would.

## The core moves

1. **Choose deliberately.** If you don't set your own priorities, someone
   else will (your boss, colleagues, clients, even family) — and you'll
   lose sight of what's actually significant.
2. **Discern aggressively.** Most effort does not produce proportional
   results; a small number of efforts matter disproportionately more than
   the rest ("no puedes subestimar la falta de importancia de
   prácticamente todo" — you can't overstate the unimportance of almost
   everything).
3. **Protect space to think.** Distinguishing the vital few from the
   trivial many requires unstructured space — "escape" isn't slacking,
   it's a precondition for good judgment.
4. **Protect sleep as an asset, not a cost.** Framed explicitly as what
   lets you contribute at your highest level, enabling you to achieve more
   in less time — not time "wasted."
5. **Use an extreme selection criterion.** "Si no es un sí evidente,
   entonces es un no evidente" — if it isn't an obvious yes, treat it as a
   clear no. Removes the middle ground of "maybe" that tends to fill up
   with non-essential commitments.
6. **Say no gracefully.** Clarity about what's essential is what makes
   saying no to everything else possible without excessive friction.
