# Investment Bucket Strategy (Security / Risk-Growth / Dream)

**Long-lived framework, short-lived examples**: the three-bucket
allocation logic is a durable personal-finance heuristic from
[[dinero-domina-el-juego]] (Tony Robbins); any specific index/fund named
alongside it (e.g. Vanguard 500, Wilshire 5000) is just an illustrative
example current as of the book's writing (2014), not a standing
recommendation.

## The three buckets

- **Security bucket** — capital that must not be put at risk; the
  "invencible, inhundible, inconquistable" ("invincible, unsinkable,
  unconquerable") framing for an all-weather allocation is referenced here
  (via a mention of Ray Dalio) but not detailed in the excerpt.
- **Risk/growth bucket** — money allocated accepting the investor "puede
  equivocarse" (can be wrong) in the short term, in exchange for long-term
  growth. National stock index funds (Vanguard 500 Index, Wilshire 5000
  Total Market Index) are given as concrete examples of vehicles for this
  bucket.
- **Dream bucket** — funded by saving a fixed percentage of income until
  specific goals are met (house, car, vacations, discretionary treats) —
  explicitly framed as a psychological/motivational bucket as much as a
  financial one.

## Mechanism tying it together

"Ganar más, gastar menos y automatizar el ahorro" (earn more, spend less,
automate savings) is the book's stated mechanism for feeding all three
buckets without relying on ongoing willpower.

## Relevance to this wiki

Complements [[sports-tech-market]] and any future `finance`-domain pages
as a general personal-finance allocation heuristic. No direct tie to
[[athletix-ai]] — this is personal investing, not startup/business
finance.

Finance cluster (expanded 2026-07-23): the Security bucket's "invincible,
unsinkable" framing is Ray Dalio's All-Weather idea — [[principios-nuevo-orden-mundial]]
now supplies the macro backdrop (high-savings / low-debt resilience across
debt cycles) that this household heuristic hedges against. And
[[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]] (Housel) reframes the whole point of saving as
buying **independence today** ("la riqueza sin independencia es una forma
peculiar de pobreza") — the *why* behind automating the buckets.
