# Enough: Hedonic Adaptation & Wealth = Have − Want

**Long-lived pattern, consolidated 2026-07-23** (flagged in
[reports/2026-07-23.md](../reports/2026-07-23.md), item 4b). Several independent
books arrive at the same conclusion: satisfaction is governed by the **gap
between what you have and what you want**, and because wanting *adapts upward* as
you acquire, chasing "more" is a treadmill. The lever is managing the denominator
(wants), not just growing the numerator (haves). Also gives the `finance` domain
a conceptual anchor beyond [[investment-bucket-strategy]].

## The shared claim, per source

- **[[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]]** (Housel) — the sharpest statement: "riqueza =
  lo que tienes menos lo que quieres." Aim for **durable satisfaction over
  fleeting happiness spikes**; "es improbable que tengas una vida buena... a menos
  que venzas tu insaciabilidad." Independence (having enough that money doesn't
  control you) is itself a form of wealth.
- **[[sapiens]]** (Harari) — the anthropology/biology underneath it: a psychological
  **happiness ceiling** (~level 7) that external gains can't durably raise, plus
  the **luxury→necessity ratchet** ("the biggest fraud in history") — comforts
  reliably become baseline expectations and generate new obligations.
- **[[esencialismo]]** (McKeown) — "less but better" and the discipline of deciding
  what is *enough* / essential, so acquisition and commitment don't fill all
  available space by default. See [[essentialism-less-but-better]].
- **[[dinero-domina-el-juego]]** (Robbins) — the mechanical side: "ganar más,
  gastar menos, automatizar el ahorro," and framing enough-to-be-free as the real
  finish line rather than an ever-rising number.

## What's convergent vs. distinct

All four locate well-being in the *want* side of the ledger, not the *have* side.
They differ in register: Housel (personal-finance psychology), Harari (historical
anthropology + happiness research), McKeown (prioritization discipline), Robbins
(savings mechanics). Compatible framings of one claim, not competing theories.

## Related tensions and threads

- The **attention/status** engine that inflates "want" is covered by
  [[internal-scorecard]] (recognition/status-seeking) and
  [[attention-economy-and-pleasure-pain]] (comparison and FOMO as addictive
  levers; the hedonism→anhedonia treadmill in [[generacion-dopamina]] is the
  neurochemical version of the same treadmill).
- Sits *against* the accumulation drive in [[piense-y-hagase-rico]] and parts of
  [[habitos-para-ser-millonario]] — though those aim at *earning* more, not
  *wanting* more, so it's a difference in emphasis, not a hard contradiction.

## Relevance to this wiki

A standing lens for any future `finance` or `reflections` ingest that frames "more
money / more stuff" as the goal — check whether the source is managing wants or
just chasing haves. Also connects to the creator work only loosely: the
"satisfaction over spikes" idea mirrors [[estrategia-contenido-absadev]]'s
"community/enjoyment over the vanity metric," but that link is thematic, not a
claim the user derived one from the other.
