# Sports-Tech Market Opportunity

Market-sizing figures cited in the [[athletix-ai]] pitch deck to frame the
opportunity (source/methodology for these numbers not given in the deck
itself — treat as claims to verify, not independently confirmed data):

- Global Sports-Tech market: USD 61.7B by 2030.
- AI in Sports market: USD 49.9B by 2033.
- Sports-Tech CAGR: 21.9%.

Framing: the sports industry is moving toward biometrics, AI, wearables,
intelligent recovery, and predictive analysis. Latin America is described
as having a large digitalization gap relative to international
organizations that already invest heavily in this technology — this gap
is the opportunity ATHLETIX AI is positioned to address.
