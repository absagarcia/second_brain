# The Internal Scorecard

**Long-lived pattern, consolidated 2026-07-23** from the 12-book batch (flagged
in [reports/2026-07-23.md](../reports/2026-07-23.md), items 4a + 5). Buffett's
term "inner scorecard" is the cleanest name: **judge yourself by an internal
standard, not by others' approval or by comparison to other people.** Five
independent sources converge on it from different angles, without citing each
other. Two faces of one idea:

## Face 1 — Silent ego: don't source your worth from recognition

- **[[atrevete-a-no-gustar]]** (Adler) — deny the desire for recognition; chasing
  praise hands your self-worth to others, so the **courage to be disliked** is the
  price of freedom. Praise itself is a *vertical* relationship ("el juicio que una
  persona capaz hace sobre una incapaz"); build horizontal ones instead.
- **[[haz-cosas-dificiles]]** (Magness) — real confidence is a **silent ego**;
  "la búsqueda de la autoestima nos predispone a tener baja autoestima" because it
  makes your worth depend on what others give you.
- **[[el-arte-de-gastar-dinero]]** (Housel) — most status spending buys *others'*
  attention, which is fast but shallow; respect earned for **who you are, not what
  you own** collapses the urge. Buffett: measure success by "cuántas de las
  personas que quieres que te quieran te quieren de verdad."

## Face 2 — Compete with your past/ideal self, not with others

- **[[de-que-hablo-cuando-hablo-de-correr]]** (Murakami) — "el contrincante... es
  el tú de ayer."
- **[[atrevete-a-no-gustar]]** (Adler) — a *healthy* inferiority feeling comes from
  comparison "con el yo ideal, no con los demás"; "la vida no es una competición."
- **[[haz-cosas-dificiles]]** (Magness) — improve your **best average**, not an
  unrepeatable peak.
- **[[mindset-la-actitud-del-exito]]** (Dweck) — the growth mindset's internal,
  trajectory-based standard ("not yet"), stretching yourself rather than proving
  you're already good. See [[growth-vs-fixed-mindset]].

## Why the two faces are one concept

Both replace an **external reference point** (applause, status, beating rivals)
with an **internal** one (your values, your prior self, your ideal self). The
external scorecard is the failure mode in both directions: it makes worth
contingent on things you don't control, and it's the exact nerve the attention
economy pulls on — see [[attention-economy-and-pleasure-pain]] (comparison and
approval-seeking as the addictive levers) and [[minimalismo-digital]] ("don't
click like").

- Directly load-bearing for the creator work. [[absa-garcia]]'s running arc bets
  on an internal-standard journey ("el rival es el tú de ayer") rather than
  chasing views, and [[estrategia-contenido-absadev]]'s diagnosis is that the
  channel leaks at *conversion*, not reach — an inner-scorecard framing of why
  optimizing for reach was the wrong target.
