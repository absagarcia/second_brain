# The CODE Method (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express)

**Long-lived framework**: Tiago Forte's core method in
[[building-a-second-brain]] for personal knowledge management — a
process, not a tool-specific or time-sensitive claim.

## The four steps

1. **Capture** — keep what resonates, not everything. Filtered by
   feeling/inspiration rather than exhaustiveness ("does it inspire me?"),
   and driven by recurring personal questions (e.g. how do I live less in
   the past and more in the present; how do I move from mindless
   consumption to mindful creation).
2. **Organize** — save for actionability rather than by source/origin;
   the excerpt doesn't detail Forte's PARA method here, only the
   principle that organization should be driven by what you'll do with
   something, not where it came from.
3. **Distill** — find the essence; "more is not better when it comes to
   thinking and creating" — compression toward the essential, not
   accumulation.
4. **Express** — show your work; "we only know what we make." Framed as
   proof that captured/distilled ideas can actually make a difference,
   and as a way to avoid starting things that never get finished by
   expressing in small units.
