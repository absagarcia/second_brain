# Mediocristán vs. Extremistán

Taleb's two-worlds distinction, from [[el-cisne-negro]]. **Long-lived** — a
classification principle, not a data point. Its practical value is as a *first
question* to ask of any metric before choosing how to reason about it.

| | **Mediocristán** | **Extremistán** |
|---|---|---|
| Example quantity | height, weight, waist size, calories | wealth, book sales, video views, startup returns |
| Effect of one observation | negligible on the total | **can dominate the total** |
| Governing curve | Gaussian / bell curve | power law / scalable |
| Effect of adding data | inequality shrinks toward the mean | "no ocurre lo mismo con lo escalable: la desigualdad permanece invariable" |
| Averages | meaningful | misleading |
| Prediction | works | doesn't |

> "En Extremistán, las desigualdades son tales que una única observación puede
> influir de forma desproporcionada en el total." (ch. 3)

## The 80/20 law and its abuse

Origin recorded correctly: **Vilfredo Pareto** observed that 80% of Italy's land
belonged to 20% of the population. Taleb immediately flags the drift — "algunos
emplean la regla para dar a entender que el 80% del trabajo lo realiza el 20% de
las personas. O que el 80% del esfuerzo incide sólo en un 20% de los resultados"
— i.e. the productivity folklore version is a *reuse* of a wealth-distribution
observation, not a finding of its own. Worth holding against
[[essentialism-less-but-better]] and [[effortless-action-principles]], both of
which lean on 80/20 as if it were established for effort. They may still be
right; the citation is not what people think it is.

## The core danger

> "Es como elaborar un medicamento para plantas y aplicárselo a los seres
> humanos. No es de extrañar que corramos el mayor de los peligros: manejamos
> asuntos que pertenecen a Extremistán, pero tratados como si pertenecieran a
> Mediocristán, como una «aproximación»." (ch. 17)

Taleb's added cynicism about why the bad tool persists: "lo que determina el sino
de una teoría en la ciencia social es el contagio, no su validez."

## Where this applies inside this wiki

- **Content metrics ([[estrategia-contenido-absadev]], [[absadev]],
  [[absa-garcia]]).** Views are the textbook Extremistán quantity. Concretely,
  [[absa-garcia]]'s 26,293 views in 28 days with **92% from World Cup content**
  is a single observation dominating the total — an average-views-per-video
  figure for that channel is close to meaningless. Same for
  [[blackicelabs-podcast]], where 471 plays across 23 episodes hide a top
  episode at 40. **Median and distribution, not mean.**
- **Startup returns ([[de-cero-a-uno]], [[unicorn-startup-status]]).** Thiel's
  power law is this same claim, reached independently and used to argue for
  concentration rather than caution.
- **Investing ([[investment-bucket-strategy]], [[dinero-domina-el-juego]],
  [[principios-nuevo-orden-mundial]]).** Long-run "average return" assumptions
  are a Mediocristán tool pointed at an Extremistán quantity; the bucket
  strategy's security bucket is, in effect, a hedge against exactly that.
- **Sports outcomes.** Match results are closer to Mediocristán than views are,
  but tournament *outcomes* are not — relevant to any prediction work under
  [[athletix-ai]].
